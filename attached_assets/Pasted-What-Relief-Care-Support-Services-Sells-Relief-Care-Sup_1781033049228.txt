What Relief Care Support Services Sells
Relief Care Support Services Limited is a professional recruitment, placement and managed domestic staffing agency. We connect families, individuals and organisations with vetted, trained and reliable domestic workers and caregivers. Our services cover the full spectrum of home and personal support including caregivers, nannies, babysitters, housekeepers, cooks, drivers, elderly care assistants, hospital sitters, physical therapy assistants, wound care support, nutrition and meal planning, errands and escort, and palliative care. In some cases we go beyond placement and directly employ and manage domestic staff on behalf of clients, handling supervision, conduct and salary payments so the client has zero administrative burden.
Who Our Customers Are
Our clients are busy families and individuals across Nigeria and in the diaspora, particularly Nigerians living in the UK, US, and Canada who need trusted, managed care for elderly parents or relatives back home. We also serve working couples who need reliable childcare, professionals who need household management, and organisations requiring support staff. Our job seekers are skilled domestic workers and caregivers across Nigeria looking for vetted, legitimate placement opportunities with good families.
Tone of Voice
Warm, trustworthy, professional and reassuring. We speak like a knowledgeable friend who understands the anxiety of letting a stranger into your home or trusting someone with your loved one. We are never corporate or cold. We lead with empathy and back it with professionalism.
What Makes Relief Care Unique
We do not just match people. We take responsibility. Our thorough vetting process, post-placement follow-up, replacement policy, and managed service model set us apart from informal referrals and untested agencies. We serve clients not just in Lagos but nationwide and remotely for the diaspora, and we offer flexible arrangements from hourly to live-in. Our tagline says it best: "Let's give you a helping hand."
